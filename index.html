    About jotBible

    jotBible is a place to read the Bible and keep your own notes and study cards alongside it.

    Read

    • Pick a book, then a chapter. Breadcrumbs at the top take you back a level.
    • Tap Bookmark under a chapter title to save your place (one per book). Find them under Bookmarks on the Read page.

    Work with verses

    • Tap one or more verses to select them, then Underline, Copy, or start a New Note or New Flashcard with them filled in.
    • A small green dot by a verse number means a note or flashcard mentions it. Select the verse to see links to them.

    Notes & Study

    • In 'Edit' modee, use the toolbar for bold and bulleted or numbered lists.
    • Type a reference like John 3:16 or Rom 8:28–30 and it becomes a link to that passage.
    • On the Study page, tap a flashcard to flip between the reference and the scripture.

    Search

    • Search the Bible and your notes at once, or narrow results with the OT, NT, and Notes filters.

    Good to know

    • Each tab remembers where you left off, and unsaved edits are kept as drafts.
    • Everything is saved on this device only. Reset jotBible erases it all.

    Reset jotBible?

    This permanently deletes everything jotBible has saved on this device: notes, flashcards, underlines, bookmarks, unsaved drafts, and downloaded Bible text. It can't be undone.
